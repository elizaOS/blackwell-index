## Contract decisions to settle from the source audit

1. Choose the exact bundle definition. An instance including local storage and an instance whose storage is billed separately do not become identical through per-GPU normalization. Preserve the distinction; do not insert an estimated storage adjustment without evidence and approval.
2. Choose minimum instance size and topology. An eight-GPU research slice can reduce size heterogeneity, but it does not resolve storage, geographic or hardware-comparability gaps by itself.
3. Define geographic coverage and availability semantics. A global or unspecified catalog tariff is not proof of a regionally executable capacity offer.
4. Review the economic-owner mapping and rights covering the legal benchmark operator and financial-reference use. Multiple SKUs, URLs or signing nodes do not establish additional independent economic sources.
5. Approve constituent weights, source-age/dispersion policy and correction/discontinuation rules. A short constant-price sample cannot calibrate leveraged-market risk.

## Pyth interface agreement needed before production code

Use the existing snapshot fields as the input inventory: `network`, `methodologyHash`, `registryHash`, `publishable`, optional `publicationScope` and the selected feed's `id`, `price`, `confidence`, `observedAt`, `calculatedAt`, `status` and reasons. These are SBX fields, **not an asserted Pyth managed-service wire format**. Agree their supported mapping with Pyth. SBX feed IDs and publication bindings remain unassigned. The upstream Base verifier addresses and reported BTC trial access are separate consumer evidence; they do not supply an SBX binding or a managed-service agreement.

The [public resolver acceptance](PYTH_HIP3_RESOLVER_ACCEPTANCE.md), [inactive B200 publication scope](MODEL_PUBLICATION_SCOPE.md) and [exact offer schedule](B200_OFFER_SCHEDULE.md) are reproducible local engineering. Ask whether Pyth's managed service supplies the custom listener or expects an extension against its public pusher. The resolver's internal price/timestamp structure alone does not bind SBX metadata or prove accepted service ingestion.

Keep original source retrieval age distinct from calculation time, Pyth generation time and heartbeat receipt. Define how the service receives an unavailable print, rejects stale inputs and confirms the value consumed by the venue. Redundant endpoints must preserve the same approved benchmark and version. Do not assume a fresh delivery timestamp proves fresh source data.

The required acceptance sequence is: approved benchmark inputs → supported authenticated Pyth ingestion → independently verified upstream receipt/value/time → actual venue oracle and mark readback → venue-approved outage and recovery exercise. Repeated, delayed, reordered and stale updates need explicit results at each boundary. Local fixtures only establish the local boundaries they exercise.

Continue using the existing [partner packet](PYTH_PARTNER_PACKET.md); it remains unsent. Provider source access, permissions, independent operation, Pyth acceptance, venue agreement and sustained operation cannot be manufactured from local test results.
